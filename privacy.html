Moonlight Mix Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Moonlight Mix stores game progress, preferences, and rewards locally on your device. This version does not create user accounts, collect precise location, access contacts, or sell personal information.

Device features

The app may use optional vibration or haptic feedback. You can disable this in Settings.

Future advertising and purchases

If advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases are added in a later release, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are enabled.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
