# Test checklist

- Select a non-empty bottle and confirm it remains lit.
- Tap a valid destination and confirm the source bottle visibly lifts, moves, tilts, and pours before the board changes.
- Confirm a colored stream and droplets enter the destination.
- Confirm controls and extra bottle taps are locked only during the animation.
- Confirm invalid destinations still shake and do not move liquid.
- Confirm undo, restart, hints, completed bottles, and the next level still work.
- Confirm the animation stays inside the screen on a small iPhone.
